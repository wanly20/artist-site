The Artist

Hi, I'm Cleo

I'm an illustrator who draws soft creatures, peculiar folk, and little worlds you can get lost in. Everything starts as a pencil sketch — the wobbly lines and unexpected shapes are the whole point.

My characters tend to find each other across different pieces — you might spot the Witch chatting with the Mushroom Mayor if you look carefully.
